Shipping Policy

Unless explicitly listed for auction, original artwork purchases and shipping are arranged by email at artwithrayan@gmail.com. Shipping costs are confirmed based on the artwork, protective packaging, and delivery address before payment. Original artwork is packed and shipped by Rayan Rao Art.

For auctions, the fixed shipping and protective packing charge is calculated from the destination state and displayed before bid authorization. This store charge is locked for an accepted bid; no additional shipping fee is added when the auction ends. Online auction shipping is limited to the contiguous United States and Washington, DC. See the Auction Rules.

Print orders are made to order and fulfilled through Printful. Shipping is calculated from the product and delivery address during checkout.

Printful prints can be shipped internationally where the selected product and destination are supported. Availability and shipping costs are checked before payment. Any self-fulfilled prints are currently limited to US delivery; international shipping for original artwork outside an auction is arranged by email.

All website prices and checkout charges are in US dollars. International customers may need to pay customs duties, import taxes, or carrier handling fees separately on delivery. These fees are not guaranteed to be included in the shipping or fulfillment-tax estimate at checkout.

Delivery estimates and carrier service may vary by product, destination, and fulfillment location. Customs processing can delay international shipments.
