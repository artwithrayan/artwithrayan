Auction Rules

Version 2026-10-07-v1

Bidding

All amounts are in US dollars. The starting price is the minimum selling price; there is no hidden reserve. Verify your email, provide a shipping address, and save a card securely with Stripe before your first bid can be accepted. You must be at least 18 and authorized to use the card.

Your maximum bid is private. Automatic bidding uses only what is needed to lead, up to your maximum. The first bid starts at the starting price. Equal maximum bids are ranked by which was accepted first. Increments are $5 below $100, $10 below $500, $25 below $1,000, and $50 thereafter. The winning amount can be less than a full increment when limited by a maximum bid.

Bids are commitments to purchase if you win. You may increase, but not lower, an accepted maximum bid online. Contact artwithrayan@gmail.com promptly about a genuine bidding error. Saving a card alone does not guarantee acceptance: the bid must still meet the minimum and arrive before the server's closing time.

Closing

A competing bid accepted during the last two minutes extends bidding until two minutes after that bid. Increasing your own leading maximum does not extend bidding. The highest accepted maximum wins at the final displayed winning price. If no bid meets the starting price, the artwork remains unsold.

Shipping And Total

Online auction bidding currently supports the contiguous United States and Washington, DC. Before authorizing a bid, you see a fixed shipping and protective packing charge calculated from your destination state. This is the store's shipping charge, not a live carrier rate. It is locked for your accepted bid even if postage later costs more. Your shipping address cannot be changed through the bidding form after an accepted bid; contact us for assistance.

Your maximum authorized total is your maximum bid plus the displayed shipping charge. Your actual winning charge is the final winning bid plus that shipping charge. There is no buyer premium, added card-processing fee, or other amount added after authorization. Any tax the seller must remit is accounted for within the agreed price, not charged beyond your authorized total.

Card Authorization

You must explicitly agree to save your card with Stripe and authorize a charge only if you win. No auction purchase payment is collected when bidding. Stripe or your bank may display a temporary card-verification authorization; it is not an auction purchase charge. Losing bidders are not charged.

When the auction closes, the winner's card is automatically charged the final winning bid plus the accepted shipping charge, never more than the agreed maximum total. The website stores Stripe references, not your full card number or security code. Saving a card does not guarantee that the bank will approve payment.

Payment Problems

If payment fails or needs bank verification, the winner is emailed a secure payment link with 24 hours to complete payment. The artwork is not marked sold until payment is confirmed. If payment remains incomplete, the seller reviews the auction; another bidder is not automatically charged. An interruption or technical problem may require seller review before the transaction can proceed.
