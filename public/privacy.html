Privacy Policy

This website collects information needed to process purchases, shipping, receipts, and customer support.

Payments are processed by Stripe. Card numbers are not stored by this website. Fulfillment and transactional email may involve Printful, Stripe, and Resend.

Information is used to operate the store, communicate about orders, and maintain transaction records.

Auction bidding also stores your verified email, shipping address, private maximum bids, accepted shipping amount, payment authorization text and time, and Stripe customer/payment-method references. Private maximums and shipping addresses are not shown publicly. An essential, seven-day sign-in cookie keeps you authenticated; you may sign out to revoke it. Verification codes expire after ten minutes.

Transactional auction emails cover sign-in, bid acceptance, outbids, results, and payment issues. Paid order details are recorded in the seller's private Google Sheets order tracker. Contact artwithrayan@gmail.com about your information; transaction and authorization records may need to be retained for order support and recordkeeping.
